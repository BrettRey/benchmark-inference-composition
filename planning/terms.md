# Terminology ledger — Minds and Machines

Read by `.house-style/check-terms.py --ledger`. A row containing "free" exempts
that term: the venue's readers own it. Everything else must be glossed at first
use. **Do not soften a gloss to pass the gate** — gloss it, or justify "free"
here. The free/earned split was decided in `submission/venue-decision-2026-08-09.md`.

The audience is philosophers of AI and cognitive science. That is a specialist
philosophy-of-science readership, which the house rule on projectibility first
mentions explicitly says "may need less" than a linguistics or public audience.
