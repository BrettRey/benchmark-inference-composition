Thank you for considering this manuscript.

The paper argues that separately warranted links in an AI evaluation argument do
not compose into a warranted chain, and supplies an interface audit that says
when they do. It engages recent work in this journal directly: Langer, Baum and
Schlicker (2025) and Buijsman (2026) on human oversight and joint performance,
Gonzalez Barman et al. (2024) on benchmarking scientific understanding, and
Bachmann et al. (2024) on psychometrics in NLP measurement. That is why it is
offered here rather than to a general AI venue.
